The Problem

GIS data is everywhere. Insights are not.

Every municipality, water board, and utility sits on hundreds of spatial datasets — but answering even simple questions takes hours.

⏳

Hours lost to SQL

A GIS analyst needs to write complex PostGIS queries for every ad-hoc spatial question. A single analysis takes 2–4 hours instead of 2 minutes.

🔒

Data locked from decision-makers

Policy officers and managers need geo insights daily but cannot access them without a GIS specialist. GIS teams are the bottleneck.

☁️

Cloud AI is not an option

Dutch government data (BGT, BAG, underground infrastructure) cannot leave the premises. ChatGPT-based tools are legally blocked for this sector.

💸

Esri owns the market

ArcGIS costs €30K–150K/year per organization. Its AI features require cloud. Dutch municipalities are actively looking for a sovereign alternative.

The Solution

Natural language → GIS analysis → map

GeoAI is an on-premise AI system that understands geospatial questions in plain Dutch or English, runs the analysis against PostGIS, and returns the result as an interactive map — no SQL, no code, no cloud.

"Show me all buildings that lose the most heat, within 200m of a gas pipeline, in the Stadshart neighbourhood."
→ GeoAI finds the right layers, runs buffer + spatial join + attribute filter, renders the result on a map, and explains: "47 buildings found. Average heat loss index 8.2. Top priority: Rembrandtweg 12–34."
